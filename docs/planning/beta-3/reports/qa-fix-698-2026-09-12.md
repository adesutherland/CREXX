# Issue 698: prevent load-dependent sanitizer timeout failures

The exact-head Sanitizer QA run for `fd866d9ef770c289e592f66d97a5c8c68b2727b3`
reported red Linux x64 and macOS arm64 jobs. Each job failed only
`binary_global_import_types (Timeout)` at its 600-second outer limit. Neither
job log contains an AddressSanitizer or LeakSanitizer diagnostic.

The unchanged Apple-ASan binary-import matrix passes serially in 305.57 seconds
at `cmake-build-debugasan/asan-logs/20260912-193456-ctest` and, after the #695
compiler candidate, in 289.27 seconds at
`cmake-build-debugasan/asan-logs/20260912-210749-ctest`. The #695 eight-way full
ASan invocation likewise completed 2,300/2,302 and timed out only that matrix
and `crexx_project_build_contract`; the normal 30-way suite exposed the same
contention class in those two tests and `crexx_process_runtime`.

These are nested end-to-end aggregate tests. They invoke many compiler,
assembler, linker, project-build, and VM operations inside one CTest. Starting
them beside the rest of the broad suite makes their outer and inner deadlines
measure host contention rather than the product path.

Recent history explains the timing change. Commit `8780af49a` expanded
`crexx_project_build.cmake` by 137 lines on 6 September. Commit `fa887be15`
introduced the 68-line process-runtime aggregate on 12 September. Commit
`3be2b83f` then added 158 lines to the binary-global matrix on 12 September.
The current unchanged tests run two to five times faster in isolated replays
than in the contended broad pool. That demonstrates a test-growth and host-load
mechanism. These CTest elapsed times do not establish a compiler or runtime
performance regression; that claim would require an identical-workload
before/after comparison.

The repair marks all three aggregates `RUN_SERIAL`. It retains every scenario
in ordinary and sanitizer gates while giving each aggregate the whole host when
CTest schedules it. The expanded binary-import and project-build matrices also
receive 900-second outer limits, providing measured margin for instrumented
hosted macOS without disabling a hang bound. Functional failures and sanitizer
diagnostics remain ordinary red test results; there is no retry, suppression,
exclusion, or `continue-on-error` path.

Qualification records the generated CTest properties and runs the three-test
aggregate panel through normal Debug and maintained Apple ASan. A fresh hosted
Sanitizer QA run must then pass both Linux ASan/LSan and macOS ASan at the exact
published commit before the affected product issues are closed.

After regeneration, both `cmake-build-debug` and
`cmake-build-debugasan` report the intended CTest properties:

- `binary_global_import_types`: `RUN_SERIAL=true`, `TIMEOUT=900`;
- `crexx_project_build_contract`: `RUN_SERIAL=true`, `TIMEOUT=900`;
- `crexx_process_runtime`: `RUN_SERIAL=true`, `TIMEOUT=600`.

The normal Debug panel was deliberately invoked with `--test-jobs 8` at
`cmake-build-debug/asan-logs/20260912-211901-ctest`. CTest admitted only one
aggregate at a time and passed 3/3: binary imports in 150.23 seconds, process
runtime in 117.97 seconds, and project build in 166.75 seconds. The complete
panel took 435.03 seconds, less than one former 600-second false timeout.

The identical eight-worker panel passed 3/3 under maintained Apple ASan at
`cmake-build-debugasan/asan-logs/20260912-212627-ctest`, again with only one
aggregate admitted at a time. Project build took 294.58 seconds, process
runtime 192.70 seconds, and binary imports 351.25 seconds; the complete panel
took 838.61 seconds and emitted no sanitizer diagnostic. The corresponding
regeneration/build logs are
`cmake-build-debug/asan-logs/20260912-211743-build` and
`cmake-build-debugasan/asan-logs/20260912-211815-build`.

`AGENTS.md` now requires isolated Debug/sanitizer timing before adding or
materially expanding nested aggregate tests. It also requires a single
unchanged isolated check for timeout-only broad failures, followed by a harness
repair before another full-suite cycle, and keeps issue splitting for findings
with distinct causes, components, repairs, approval paths, or cross-cutting
impact. Compatible unpublished repairs should share one exact-head hosted gate.
