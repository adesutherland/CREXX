PERF3-13 Gate E E6 accepted C0 closeout

Decision
  Adrian accepted strict C0 ownership and authorized closure on 2026-08-14.
  C1 automatic quiescent reclaim and the C2 test-only remote-free queue are
  removed. Gate F implementation and public workers/channels remain closed.

Selected production form
  - Uniform owner-token enforcement precedes worker-owned standard, extent and
    value-array allocation, resize and release mutation.
  - A foreign thread with empty allocator TLS is rejected when it passes an
    explicit worker.
  - Final audit also closes value-array overflow accounting before it can
    mutate owner-local failure telemetry.
  - Wrong-owner allocation, resize and free telemetry is synchronized in the
    memory context; successful owner-local allocation/free remains lock-free.
  - The existing one-empty-slab-per-class and depot-return policy is unchanged.
  - Permanent private coverage exercises spin and allocation churn at
    1/2/4/8 workers on rxvml, rxbvml and rxtvml and asserts exact maximum
    parallelism.

Rejected comparisons
  - C1: exact retained allocator slabs fall by up to 38.620690%, but external
    peak RSS is inconclusive and rxtvml 8-worker churn has a clear +3.710323%
    post-quiescent current-RSS cost. The one -4.476433% throughput guard does
    not reproduce in exact confirmation (+1.729879%, crossing interval).
  - C2: +183.669864% mean elapsed, 95% interval +179.250161% to +188.089568%,
    0/12 favourable pairs, neutral/inconclusive peak RSS and no required
    lifetime after receiver materialization.
  - Recorded comparative evidence: 568/568 processes passed.
