UNICODE-NORM-02 prepared-symbol NFD recovery — 2026-08-27

Scope
  Indicative algorithm screen on the unicode research branch. This is not a
  clean-host product-format verdict and does not select an implicit string API.

What was recovered
  The previously performance-green implementation had remained uncommitted in
  /tmp/crexx-unicode-nfd-signal.5E5rDu/repo. It iterates CREXX .string
  codepoints, reads one dense portable at-u32 prepared symbol per scalar, and
  dispatches exact synthetic kinds in a shared Level B executor. The slower
  committed successor instead generated a 3,440-state UTF-8 byte DFA. Only the
  Rexx/Level L recovery was applied; no scratch compiler C change was retained.

Interpretation
  The previous 8–100x regression belonged to the rejected byte-DFA successor,
  not to the prepared-symbol codepoint algorithm. The recovered algorithm is
  again at least at parity for call-heavy short rows and is materially faster
  on sustained buffers, approaching twice canonical throughput at 812 KB and
  6.50 MB. Variable load makes the exact percentages provisional, but cannot
  plausibly account for the disappearance of the old order-of-magnitude loss.
